Models & libraries

Active angle model: MoGe-2 Small, normal-only graph, learned weights unchanged. MIT and DINOv2 notices · Manifest and provenance · Reproducible conversion. Raw normals estimate tilt inside fixed RGB faces; they do not define the boundaries.

FreeClimbs YOLO hold detector, © 2024 John LaRocque, AGPL-3.0. Fixed 960px graph adaptation, learned weights unchanged. Conversion source · License.

Metric3D ViT Small ONNX export (legacy research asset, not active). Unmodified publisher FP16 weights, split for delivery. Publisher declaration: CC0-1.0. Upstream Metric3D code: BSD-2-Clause, © 2024 Wei Yin and Mu Hu. Full notices · Model manifest · Preparation source.
